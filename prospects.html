02

What they are here for

Everyone who has met Pinned but not sent anything: group members, enquirers, abandoned quotes, and every receiver who has only ever collected.

The job they hire Pinned for

Find out, in one line, whether Pinned solves their problem.

Every message on this page is judged against that sentence. If it does not help with it, or protect it, it should not be sent.

The hook
TriggerA reply in the group, or an SMS an hour after they checked a price.
ActionGet a quote. Ten seconds, no account.
RewardA real price and a real time, instantly — not a callback.
InvestmentThe quote keeps their route; signing up is one field.
03

Touchpoints

Groups

P1

Marketplace group reply

Three sentences: proof, price-and-speed, link. The fourth sentence is what makes it an advert.

 Not builtWhatsApp
TriggerA delivery question in a Nairobi buy-and-sell or courier group
TimingWithin the hour, by a human
SourceNew · acquisition
Hook · Trigger
WhatsApp200 chars · 2 segments
We deliver across Nairobi — drop at any Pinned pod, or a runner collects from your shop. Same-day inside town, and the receiver gets a code to collect. Price in ten seconds, no account: pinnedflow.app
Rules
  • Never post a star rating or an invented review count. Only figures the live board can prove.
  • One link, no emoji wall, no all-caps. Groups mute sellers who shout.
  • Reply to the question that was asked before describing the service. A reply that ignores the question is an advert.

Enquiry

P2

First reply to an enquiry

Ask for the two things that produce a price. Nothing else.

 Not builtWhatsApp
TriggerA direct message or call-back request
TimingWithin 5 minutes in working hours
SourceNew · acquisition
Hook · Action
WhatsApp108 chars · 1 segment
Hi — Pinned here. Tell me the pickup area and the drop area and I'll send the price and the time right away.
Rules
  • Two questions maximum in a first reply. Every extra question halves the replies.
  • Never open with 'Thank you for contacting Pinned'. It is the sentence people scroll past.

Quote

P3

You checked a price

The quote already did the persuading. Repeat it exactly and get out of the way.

 Not builtSMS
TriggerA quote is generated on the web and not booked
TimingOnce, within the hour
SourceNew · acquisition
Hook · Trigger
SMS152 chars · 1 segment
You checked a Pinned price: {{origin}} to {{dest}}, Ksh {{amount}}, about {{eta}}. Still available: {{link}} Reply STOP to opt out.
Rules
  • Promotional, so it carries an opt-out — the code already refuses to save promotional copy without one.
  • Once. There is no second message in this sequence, ever.
  • Repeat the exact figures they saw. A different number reads as a bait and switch.

01

What they are here for

The job they hire Pinned for

Change the plan without making anyone feel blamed or lost.

Every message on this page is judged against that sentence. If it does not help with it, or protect it, it should not be sent.

The hook
TriggerA human decision, pushed to the person it affects within seconds.
ActionAccept the new state, or say what is happening.
RewardBeing told early, before it becomes their problem.
InvestmentRunners who are told the truth about reassignments keep taking offers.

The SMS journey

Every moment in order, and the channel that carries it. Highlighted rows cost an SMS: 4 on a journey that works, 0 more only when something goes wrong. A row numbered + is one of those; everything unhighlighted is a deliberate decision not to spend an SMS.

Four SMS, all of them about somebody's income

Every message in this lane is a human changing a runner's plan. Each one answers the two questions a runner actually has: did I do something wrong, and can I work now.

Reassignment

A1a

Job reassigned — previous runner

Remove the two fears at once: did I do something wrong, and am I owed anything.

SMS137 chars · 1 segment
{{batch}} has been reassigned to another runner. Nothing is owed from your side and your rating is unchanged. New offers continue as normal.
In-app
{{batch}} reassigned

Reason: {{reason}}. Your rating and standing are unchanged.

Rules
  • Say 'your rating is unchanged' explicitly. Silence on that point is read as a penalty.
  • Reassignment messages never imply fault unless ops has actually recorded fault — and then it is a conversation, not an SMS.

Availability

A3

You have gone offline

A system-detected state change, stated as fact, with the one-tap undo.

 Not builtSMS
TriggerA runner's status goes offline
TimingAt the state change
SourceMap · Admin 3
Hook · Action
SMS116 chars · 1 segment
You are offline on Pinned — no job offers until you are back on. If that wasn't you, open the app and tap Go online.
Rules
  • Most offline events are a dead battery or a lost connection, not a choice. The message assumes the accident.
  • Once per offline event. A runner who meant to go offline does not need a second SMS.

Rules

01

What they are here for

The job they hire Pinned for

Run the counter without surprises, and see the money each week.

Every message on this page is judged against that sentence. If it does not help with it, or protect it, it should not be sent.

The hook
Trigger'A runner is 12 minutes away for 4 parcels.'
ActionHave them at the counter, scan, release the code.
RewardThe weekly settlement figure — the number they open the email for.
InvestmentBranch defaults, cut-off times and handover habits that make the station faster each week.

In-app
Ksh {{amount}} settled

{{stops}} parcels, week to {{deadline}}. Statement in Settlements.

Rules
  • The amount goes in the subject line. A settlement email that makes a host open it to learn the number is a worse email.
  • The reward is weekly and variable — this is the host hook. It should never be late, and never silent in a zero week; a zero week gets its own line: 'No parcels this week, nothing to settle.'

Rules
  • 'No heartbeat' is our word. The host's word is 'offline', and what they need to know is that customers cannot collect.
  • Give the phone number in full. A host standing next to a dead pod is not going to open a portal.

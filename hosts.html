What they are here for

The job they hire Pinned for

Run the counter without surprises, and see the money each week.

Every message on this page is judged against that sentence. If it does not help with it, or protect it, it should not be sent.

The hook
Trigger'A runner is 12 minutes away for 4 parcels.'

InvestmentBranch defaults, cut-off times and handover habits that make the station faster each week.

The SMS journey

Every moment in order, and the channel that carries it. Highlighted rows cost an SMS: 1 on a journey that works, 3 more only when something goes wrong. A row numbered + is one of those; everything unhighlighted is a deliberate decision not to spend an SMS.

One SMS to open the counter — email does the teaching

A host is a business at a counter. SMS is reserved for the two things that stop the counter working: a failed delivery they must decide about, and a dead pod. The operation itself is taught by email, once, and stated in the portal every time after.

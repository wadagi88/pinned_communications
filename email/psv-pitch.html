Your shelf is full of parcels that arrived on time.

Every one of them has an owner who doesn’t know it landed. A parcel leaves your upcountry branch on the 7am and reaches your Nairobi Parcel Office by midday. Your part is done. Then it sits. Two unanswered calls, three days — and a customer angry at you for a parcel you moved on time.

That shelf isn’t a transport problem. It’s a notification problem, then a last-mile one. Both happen in Nairobi. Both are what we do.

 

The notification does a job on its own.

As soon as it arrives, the receiver gets a live page: your office, your hours, your reference. No app. They collect as always, or pay a runner to take it to their door. Either way, they stop ringing your desk.

How it works at your desk

01 Your system keeps working as it is. Parcel software, receipt book or ledger — nothing changes. We use the sender and receiver details you already hold, and send the notification to them.
02 The receiver gets it, and chooses. Agree to delivery, or collect from your office. Either way they have answered — and your clerk has stopped chasing.
03 If they agree, a rider is allocated. From that moment you track it 100%: runner accepted, picked up, in transit, delivered — every state timestamped from your counter.
04 At the door, a code closes it. The receiver gives the rider a six-digit code. The parcel is released only against that code, and the delivery is closed on the record.

What if the receiver never answers? The parcel stays with you, exactly as it does today. Nothing is lost by notifying.

Do you train us? We come to you. An hour with your clerks, and we stay through your first busy morning.

Your route stays yours — and this costs you nothing.

We do not quote Town to Town or Intercounty parcel movement, carry it, or sell against it. We are not a courier — we are the layer on top of one. The parcel stays yours until it reaches your Nairobi Parcel Office. The receiver pays for the last mile, and only if they choose the door.

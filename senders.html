Senders

Anyone who books a parcel — an individual in the app, or a business through a virtual host.

01

What they are here for

The job they hire Pinned for

Know it is handled, and be able to prove it was.

Every message on this page is judged against that sentence. If it does not help with it, or protect it, it should not be sent.

The hook
TriggerBooking confirmation lands before they have left the pod counter.
ActionDrop the parcel, or tap once to send a runner instead.
RewardProof of delivery with a name, a time and a photo — the moment that makes the next send automatic.
InvestmentSaved addresses and repeat receivers; the second send is three taps.
